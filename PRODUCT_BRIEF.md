# AtriaWall AI Product Brief

## Positioning

AtriaWall AI is a high-end gallery wall planning studio for homeowners who want the final wall to look intentional, measured, and personal. The product should feel more like an interior stylist plus installation assistant than a simple drag-and-drop collage tool.

## Reference App Learnings

The reference app offers useful basics: folders, custom frames, wall organization, swiping comparisons, exports, templates, AI layout help, and a hanging guide. AtriaWall AI expands the idea with a cleaner premium product structure, stronger layout math, richer editor controls, and a more deliberate AI workflow.

## Differentiators

- AI plans become editable frame layouts, not just advice.
- Every frame has real dimensions, mat width, nail offset, notes, lock state, and optional imported art.
- Hanging guide calculates nail marks from left/top reference points.
- Templates are curated by visual intent: editorial salon, quiet grid, staircase rise, anchor triptych.
- The app opens directly into the tool, not a marketing landing page.
- Subscription plan is intentionally simple: weekly, monthly, yearly.
- Secrets are not committed. Gemini and RevenueCat are configured locally.

## MVP Features Implemented

- Project library with sample projects and local persistence
- Wall dimension editor
- Interactive wall canvas
- Draggable frames
- Frame inspector
- Photo import into frame artwork
- Template application
- AI layout generator with Gemini REST integration and local fallback
- Hanging guide with shareable text
- AR preview screen with ARKit camera surface
- Custom RevenueCat paywall
- Unit tests for hanging math and AI clamping
- GitHub Actions simulator build/test workflow

## Premium Roadmap

- True AR anchoring: detect vertical plane, place frame nodes at physical scale, save anchor snapshots
- Perspective correction from a wall photo
- Room photo segmentation and wall boundary detection
- Print-size recommendations from uploaded photos
- Shopping list with frame sizes and mat suggestions
- PDF cut sheet export
- iCloud sync
- Multi-wall folders and before/after compare
- App Store screenshot generation once final assets are supplied

## Visual Direction

High-end, warm, editorial, quiet. Avoid cartoon DIY styling. The app should feel like a premium interior design tool: tactile paper tones, ink typography, warm metal accents, sage/blue supporting colors, and restrained controls.
